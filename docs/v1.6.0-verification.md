## What changed since v1.5.4

The registry data model gained four fields:

- `classification` — `circulating` | `fund` | `settlement` | `indexation`,
  on every active entry
- `numeric_reused` — boolean, on every active entry
- `source_url` — string, on every active and withdrawn entry
- `code_lifetime` — `{from, to}` object, on every withdrawn entry

The schema moved from `1.3.0` to `1.4.0` to declare these fields.
The registry moved from `1.5.4` to `1.6.0` to carry them.

Six new validation rules enforce the field shapes. All four wrappers
expose the new fields plus a `by_classification()` method. The shared
cross-language fixture gained a `classification_tests` block.

## Known gaps

- `code_lifetime.from` is `null` for all 135 withdrawn entries. No ISO
  assignment date is documented for any of them. The field is present
  and honest; it is not populated. See `docs/PROVENANCE.md`.
- Wrappers do not treat `classification` as required at their own
  layer — they read absent as `null`. The registry always carries it.
  This keeps the wrappers loadable against v1.5.x JSON.
- The `is_independent` default divergence between Python/CSV (`False`)
  and JS/Go/Rust (`True`) noted in earlier Phase reviews is unchanged.
  It is latent because no test vector omits the field. Deferred to a
  future phase.
