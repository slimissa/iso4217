## The three-layer model is complete

| Layer | Artifacts | Reads |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| RAW | `iso4217.json` | — |
| CURATED | SQL ×4, CSV ×4, `iso4217.parquet`, `iso4217.countries.parquet` | RAW |
| AGGREGATED | `currencies_by_region.parquet`, `pegs_summary.parquet`, `coverage_timeline.parquet` | CURATED + snapshot + CHANGELOG |

Every layer has a generator, a `--check` mode, and a CI job. The
rule that CURATED reads RAW and AGGREGATED reads CURATED is
enforced by construction — the aggregated tool cannot open
`iso4217.json`.
